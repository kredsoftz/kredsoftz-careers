Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

KredSoft Careers ("KredSoft", "we", "us") is a free career-support service for Banking, NBFC and Insurance professionals, operated as a venture of Kredsoftz. This policy explains what personal data we receive, why, how we protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to careers.kredsoftz.in, its pages (including Apply, Learn and Resume Builder), and our WhatsApp community and email.

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, along with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and related rules, as applicable.

2. Our simple approach

  • Our website does not store your data. Our forms have no database. Your details reach us only when you press send on WhatsApp or email.
  • The Resume Builder works on your device. Your details, photo and signature stay in your own browser. They are never uploaded to us.
  • We never sell your data and never charge candidates any fee.

3. What we may receive

Only what you choose to send us, for example:

  • Identity & contact: name, mobile number, email address, city.
  • Professional details: current organisation, designation, experience, products handled, role applied for, notice period and salary details (if you share them).
  • Documents: your CV, and any photo or signature included in it.
  • Employer details (for hiring partners): company name, contact person, roles and number of openings.

Please do not send Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details or passwords unless a verified employer formally asks for them during hiring.

4. Why we use it

  • To understand your profile and match you with suitable openings and hiring drives.
  • To contact you about your application, interviews, or new opportunities, by call, WhatsApp or email.
  • To share your profile with a hiring employer, only after you agree to be considered for that role.
  • To respond to employers who want to hire through us.
  • To meet legal obligations, if any.

5. Sharing

We share your details only:

  • With employers: banks, NBFCs or insurers hiring for a role, and only with your consent for that role.
  • With service providers we use to communicate (for example WhatsApp and our email provider), who process data under their own security and privacy terms.
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority.

6. WhatsApp community

Our community is set up so that members cannot see each other's numbers in the announcements group. Please don't post personal details publicly in any group. Messages you send us directly are handled as described in this policy.

7. Cookies & device storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The Resume Builder saves a draft in your browser's local storage so you don't lose work; you can erase it anytime with "Start over" or by clearing your browser data. Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

8. How long we keep data

We keep candidate details only as long as needed to help you find a role, generally up to 12 months after your last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires otherwise.

9. Security

Our website uses HTTPS encryption. We limit access to candidate data to our team, use secure email, and delete CVs that are no longer needed. No system is perfectly secure, so please share only what is necessary.

10. Your rights

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update it.
  • Ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing is as easy as giving it: just write to us.
  • Nominate a person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Email careers@kredsoftz.in with the subject "Privacy request". We aim to act within 7 days.

11. Age

Our services are meant for people aged 18 and above. If we learn that we've received details from someone under 18, we will delete them.

12. External links

Our pages may link to employers' official websites or our WhatsApp community. Their own privacy policies apply there.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with the "Last updated" date.
