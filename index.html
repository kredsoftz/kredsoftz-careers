Career guide

Prepare like an insider

Short, practical prep notes from people who have sat on both sides of the interview table.

📞 Cracking the telephonic interview
  • Take the call somewhere quiet and keep your CV, a pen and your numbers in front of you.
  • Open with a 60-second pitch: current role, product, your best monthly number, why you want to move.
  • Quote figures with confidence: monthly disbursal, file count, ticket size, DSA count.
  • Close by asking the next step and timeline. It shows you're serious.
🏠 LAP sales (RO / BM): questions you will be asked
  • Your average ticket size, monthly disbursal target vs achievement, and login-to-disbursal ratio.
  • How many active DSAs/connectors you work with, and how you source direct files.
  • Property types you've funded (residential, commercial, industrial) and common legal/technical rejections.
  • How you handle a file stuck in credit. Show you understand FOIR, LTV and end-use.
🪙 Gold loan roles: what interviewers check
  • Walk-in conversion, top-up and renewal tactics, and how you retain customers at renewal.
  • Basics of purity testing, LTV norms and auction process awareness.
  • Branch hygiene: cash, vault and audit discipline.
📊 Credit & underwriting roles
  • Be ready to assess a sample case: income, obligations, banking, bureau and property.
  • Explain your approach to self-employed income assessment and cash-flow based lending.
  • Know your delinquency numbers and how you'd spot early warning signals.
🛡️ Insurance & bancassurance roles
  • Share your premium numbers (APE/NOP), persistency, and the partner branches you managed.
  • Show how you build relationships with branch staff and run activation drives.
  • Be clear on mis-selling safeguards. Interviewers value ethical selling.
📄 A CV that gets shortlisted
  • One or two pages. Put product, location and numbers in the top 5 lines.
  • Use achievements, not duties: "₹2.4 Cr LAP disbursal/month, 112% of target".
  • Mention notice period, current CTC and preferred locations clearly.
🗂️ Keep these ready before you apply
  • Last 3 salary slips, offer/appointment letter, and relieving letters.
  • Incentive statements or performance letters, if you have them.
  • PAN, Aadhaar and educational certificates, to share only when an employer asks formally.
